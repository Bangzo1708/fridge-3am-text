A one-line text message as if a smart fridge texted its owner at 3 AM — entered for the make.fun bounty "The 3AM Fridge Text" ($2 USDC). Three candidate messages were drafted and scored against the brief (on-topic, specific and surprising, self-aware appliance voice, lands in one line); the strongest — the one that catches its owner red-handed and keeps the secret anyway — is written to the entry file:

> 3AM. Your midnight snack is a pickle straight from the jar. I didn't say anything. I am a fridge, not a snitch. Mostly.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
